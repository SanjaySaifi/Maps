Spatially Guided AR

Turn a georeferenced printed map into a live You-Are-Here interface. The whole printed sheet is tracked as the physical reference frame; the geographic map area is calibrated separately within that sheet.

1 · Geographic reference

Scan a QR code containing the map extent, or enter the four geographic corners manually. A QR code containing only a website URL will not provide the extent; in that case, use manual entry.

2 · Prepare the physical sheet

Place the complete printed sheet on a flat surface. Keep all four sheet corners visible, avoid glare, and fill most of the camera view. The QR code, legend and margins remain part of the tracked sheet.

Camera and GPS require HTTPS (or localhost). Use a modern Android Chrome browser for the first field test.

3 · Tracking method in this prototype

This version captures a reference image of the complete sheet, rectifies it using four user-selected sheet corners, then uses OpenCV ORB feature matching and a RANSAC homography to track the sheet in subsequent camera frames. The geographic map boundary is selected separately on the rectified sheet.

This is a prototype: feature tracking can be lost with blur, glare, occlusion, low texture or major lighting changes. GPS accuracy also limits the real-world precision of the marker.
